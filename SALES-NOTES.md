### Review themes
- **Positive:** "Very honest repair shop that does quality work", "stand behind their work", "always gets me the best pricing", and "will fix your car and if he can't fix it he'll send you to somebody who can". These quotes come from older listings (around 2019) and are used on the site.
- **Negative (recent):** One customer waited 2 hours without updates. Another said a power-window repair introduced a new problem.
  - **Pitch angle:** the site's text-a-quote form and clear hours help with communication.

## Pitch talking points
1. **"Right now, when someone Googles you, there's no website. On Yellow Pages and MapQuest, your 'website' link goes to a taxi company."** Show them on your phone.
2. They have a **4.5★ rating from 170 Google reviews**, which is strong social proof that no web page is showing off yet.
3. **Inspection traffic:** "inspection near me" searches are high-intent, and the site has an inspection section built to rank for them.
4. On a phone, the site offers one-tap call and directions plus a free-quote text form. Most of their customers will be on phones.
5. The **conflicting phone numbers** across directories confuse customers. Offer to clean up and claim their Google, Yelp, Birdeye and Yellow Pages listings as an add-on.
6. Hosting can be **free**, with only a domain at about $12 a year, such as `bostonservicesraleigh.com`.

## Confirm with the owner before launch
- [ ] Which phone number is the main line? Should the 706-5300 alternate line stay on the site?
- [ ] Are the hours still Mon–Fri 10–6? Are they open on Saturdays?
- [ ] Owner or mechanic name and how many years in business, for an "About" blurb
- [ ] The full list of services: AC, transmission, diagnostics, alignments? Do they do emissions?
- [ ] Do they accept walk-ins for inspections, or is an appointment needed?
- [ ] Payment methods, and whether they offer a warranty on repairs
- [ ] **Photos:** I couldn't access their photos online because Google and Facebook block them. When you visit, take photos of the storefront and sign, the bays, the tire rack and the team. Adding them to the hero and a gallery will make a big difference.
- [ ] Permission to quote their Google reviews by first name
