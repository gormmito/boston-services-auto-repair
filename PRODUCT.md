# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary:** Raleigh-area drivers, many on a phone, who need an NC state inspection before renewing their tag, need a cheap used or new tire, or have a car problem (brakes, noise, warning light, power window) and want an honest local mechanic near downtown / Capital Blvd.
- **Secondary (pitch audience):** the shop owner, who will see this site in person as a sales demo. It has to look unmistakably like *their* shop.

## Product Purpose
A one-page site for Boston Services Auto Repair, an independent mechanic, used and new tire shop, and licensed NC inspection station. Today the shop has no website: its Yellow Pages and MapQuest "website" link points to an unrelated taxi company. Success means a visitor can call, get directions, or request a quote within seconds, and the owner recognizes their own building, signs and truck.

## Positioning
A neighborhood shop in a red, white and blue former service station on Capital Blvd. It does inspection, repair and used tires in one stop. Reviewers repeatedly praise its honesty and fair prices, and the owner refers jobs elsewhere when the shop can't do them.

## Operating Context
- Address: 1702 Capital Blvd, Raleigh, NC 27604 (East Raleigh, inside the Beltline). The nearest GoRaleigh stop is "Capital Blvd at Fenton St", about a 3-minute walk.
- Hours: Mon–Fri 10am–6pm, closed Saturday and Sunday (per Google, Birdeye and other directories).
- Main phone: (919) 524-4579. This number is painted on the shop's own truck. Directories also list (919) 706-5300.
- The canopy signs read "USED TIRE" and "INSPECTIONS". The building sign reads "BOSTON SERVICES — AUTO REPAIR CENTER". The box truck reads "Boston Service Auto Repair · Tire Shop", with services "New & Used Tire Sale Here", Oil Change, NC Inspections, Brakes, Tune-ups & More.
- Google lists wheelchair-accessible parking.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services confirmed by public listings and the shop's own signage: NC safety inspections (licensed and certified station), oil changes, brakes, suspension, tune-ups, new and used tires, and free quotes.
- Unconfirmed, ask the owner: diagnostics, electrical work, AC, alignments, emissions availability, walk-in policy, payment methods and warranty terms.
- North Carolina emissions testing in Wake County is being phased out pending final EPA approval (2026). Safety inspections remain required statewide. Do not publish fees.
- Stack: static HTML, CSS and JS with no build step, hostable on GitHub Pages.

## Brand Commitments
- The real-world identity is red, white and blue: a red canopy and fascia, a white band, and blue lower walls and blue roll-up bay doors.
- The truck carries a red "B" emblem that resembles the Boston Red Sox mark. The user chose an **original** B emblem in the shop's colors instead of copying it.
- The shop's own name varies between "Boston Services" (sign, Google) and "Boston Service" (truck, Yellow Pages). Use "Boston Services Auto Repair".

## Product Principles
1. Their building is the brand: show the real place first.
2. Call, directions and quote are always one tap away on a phone.
3. Every claim is sourced. Anything unconfirmed is phrased as "call us".
4. Plain, friendly, honest voice, like the reviews.
