NC Inspections

Inspection due? We're a licensed NC station.

North Carolina requires an annual inspection before you can renew your registration. Boston Services is a licensed and certified NC inspection center, so you can get it done right here on Capital Blvd — and if anything fails, we can quote and fix it on the spot instead of sending you somewhere else.

  • Annual safety inspection — lights, brakes, steering, tires, horn, wipers, mirrors & more
  • Emissions (OBD) testing for vehicles that still require it*
  • Window tint check
  • Failed somewhere else? Bring it in for a second look and a free repair quote

*North Carolina is phasing out emissions testing in Wake County and other counties pending final federal approval. Safety inspections are still required statewide. Inspection fees are set by the state — call us for current requirements.
