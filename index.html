Inspection due? This is an official NC inspection station.

North Carolina requires a yearly inspection before you can renew your registration. Get it done here. If something fails, we can quote the repair right here instead of sending you across town.

  • Yearly safety inspection: lights, brakes, steering, tires, horn, wipers, mirrors and more
  • Failed somewhere else? Bring it in for a second look and a free repair quote

Bring: your vehicle and your registration card. Pull in Monday–Friday, 10 AM–6 PM.

Need an emissions test or a window-tint check? Call ahead to ask. North Carolina is phasing out emissions testing in Wake County, pending final federal approval. Safety inspections are still required statewide. Fees are set by the state.
